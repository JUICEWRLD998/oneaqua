Method and sources

Firstline is an independent prototype, not the OneAquaHealth Decision Support System and not endorsed by the consortium. It encodes CC-BY 4.0 content from the D2.4 Catalogue of measures (Dias, Serra, Feio, 2025). The engine owns every verdict; the proposer only reads notes.

  • R1 Fix the leaks first It is like fixing the leaks in a bucket before trying to fill it.D2.4 · p.19 · R1 Fix the leaks first
  • R1a Parallel delivery where urban constraints require implemented once essential conditions are secured or, where urban constraints require, in parallel as part of an integrated delivery strategyD2.4 · p.20 · R1a Parallel delivery where urban constraints require
  • R2 Barrier exception Barrier removal to restore longitudinal connectivity, or the implementation of fish passages in systems where barriers constitute the dominant limiting factor; in such cases, improving longitudinal connectivity becomes essential and should be prioritised at early stages of rehabilitationD2.4 · p.20 · R2 Barrier exception
  • R3 Symptom only: invasive species control otherwise, invasives simply recolonize the restored sites.D2.4 · p.21 · R3 Symptom only: invasive species control
  • R4 Compensatory measures are a last resort compensatory measures should be regarded as options of last resort, to be implemented only when it is not possible to restore or re-establish self-sustaining ecological resilience and natural processes that support long-term river recoveryD2.4 · p.21 · R4 Compensatory measures are a last resort
  • R5 A package, not a single measure They also stress that multiple problems require a package of hierarchical measures, it is not enough to just reshape the channel, nor is it enough to only plant trees.D2.4 · p.19 · R5 A package, not a single measure
  • R6 SMART objectives Once restoration targets are established, they should be translated into clear SMART objectives, Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, and Time-bound, explicitly linked to project goals and expected outcomes.D2.4 · p.21 · R6 SMART objectives
  • R7 Respect the response lag The frequency and duration of monitoring should align with the life histories of the target organisms and the expected timescale of their responses (i.e., the lag period)D2.4 · p.22 · R7 Respect the response lag
